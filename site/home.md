# The Palimpsest Path

**An asychronous community chalk art project on the Port Cygnet Boardwalk**

> **Back for the Cygnet Folk Festival — Friday 8 to Sunday 10 January 2027.** Chalk steps will appear on the boardwalk in the weeks before, and the chalk station will be open over the festival weekend.

Join me in a community art experiment.

The Palimpsest Path is a community chalk art project on a 60-metre section of the Port Cygnet Boardwalk. Walkers (that's you) are invited to follow chalk dance steps, respond to a question on the chalkboard, and add your own marks to an ever-changing shared surface. Rain might wash it clean, but like eensy the spider, we'll just start again

---

## How it works

Chalk dance steps mark a 60-metre section of the Cygnet Boardwalk. At the chalk station, you'll find coloured chalk and an invitation to follow the steps, respond to the question, or simply add a mark — a word, a drawing, a hand traced onto the rail, a ribbon connecting your story to someone else's.

There is no pressure to make something permanent or beautiful. What you add matters in that moment, to whoever walks past next. The surface remembers everything, and forgets everything, in its own time.

## Over the festival

Walk the boardwalk, find the steps, and see what other people have left since you last passed. If you'd like to hear about the next time the path is chalked, please [get in touch](/contact).
