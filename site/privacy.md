# Privacy

## The short version

This project collects no personally identifiable data from walkers. There are no cameras watching the path and no sensors on the boardwalk. If you submit a photo through this site, you control what happens to it and can request removal at any time.

---

## Photographs of the chalk surface

The project lead photographs the chalk surface each day to keep a record of what appears and what the rain washes away. These photographs are of the boardwalk surface — chalk marks, drawings, and text contributed by walkers.

Photographs are framed on the chalk, not on people. **If a person appears incidentally in a photograph**, the photo is retaken or their face is blurred before the image is published to this site.

If you are concerned that you may appear in a photograph, please [contact us](/contact).

---

## Photo submissions

If you submit a photo through the [Participate](/participate) page, the following applies:

- Your photo will be reviewed by the project lead before publication
- Your first name or nickname (if provided) may be displayed alongside your photo
- Your photo may be used in academic research related to this project
- You may request removal of your submission at any time by [contacting us](/contact)
- By submitting, you confirm that the photo does not contain images of identifiable individuals without their consent

We do not sell, share, or otherwise distribute submitted photos to third parties.

---

## The QR code and this website

Scanning the QR code at the chalk station takes you to this website. No identifying information is collected when you scan the QR code or visit this site beyond standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, page visited, timestamp) that are retained by the hosting provider, Bluehost, under their standard privacy policy.

---

## Implied consent and participation

By contributing to the chalk surface on the boardwalk, you are participating in a community art project. Your contribution may be photographed for a digital archive and used in academic research. No personal identifying information is collected in this process.

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may walk through the section without contributing, without scanning the QR code, and without engaging with the chalk station.

---

## Contact and removal requests

For any privacy-related questions or to request removal of a submitted photo, please use the [contact page](/contact) or email the project lead directly.
