# Contact

## Get in touch

The Palimpsest Path returns for the Cygnet Folk Festival, Friday 8 to Sunday 10 January 2027.

I am happy to hear from walkers, curious neighbours, journalists, researchers, or anyone who has a question, a concern, or would like to express support for the project.

**Response time:** I check messages every few days. If your matter is urgent, please indicate that in your message.

---

## What you can contact me about

- Questions about the project or how it works
- Expressions of community support for the project
- Requests to hear about the next time the path is chalked
- Concerns about the chalk station or the chalk on the boardwalk
- Requests to remove a submitted photo from the gallery
- Privacy questions
- Media enquiries
- Collaboration or replication of the project elsewhere
